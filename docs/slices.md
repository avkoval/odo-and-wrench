## Glossary

- **car** — one vehicle the owner maintains; one event stream per car
- **schedule item** — a recurring maintenance job of one car (engine oil, cabin filter) with an interval in km and/or months; whichever comes first makes it due
- **odometer reading** — the mileage shown on the instrument cluster at a moment, confirmed by the owner; usually from a photo
- **diary entry** — one visit or job done on a date at a mileage, covering one or more schedule items and/or free-text work
- **due status** — ok | soon (inside the soon window) | overdue (a limit reached or passed) | unknown (never done, no history) — computed, never stored
- **soon window** — how early an item counts as "soon" — by default 1000 km or 30 days before it is due; set in the reminder settings, and per schedule item where an item needs more notice (D5)
- **issue** — a one-off defect to fix (left turn signal, weak handbrake); open until a diary entry fixes it or the owner withdraws it; open issues can be shared with the mechanic as one message (D14, D15)
- **reminder** — a phone notification sent once per item and status level, at the time of day set in the reminder settings
- **VIN** — the 17-character vehicle identification number; decoded into maker (always) and model, year, engine (where the VIN carries them) (D10)
- **schedule template** — a ready list of schedule items for one kind of car (make, model, years, engine), shipped with the app as data; applying it defines the items in one step (D8); an add-on template (LPG system) adds the items of extra equipment to any car that has it
- **receipt lines** — the parts and labour of a garage receipt on a diary entry — name, quantity, unit, price, sum (D17)
- **receipt import** — a receipt transcribed by an AI agent into the JSON format of docs/receipt-format.md and pasted into the app (D18)
- **template import** — a schedule template made by an AI agent from the car's owner's manual in the format of docs/template-format.md, pasted into the app and kept on the phone (D21)
- **cost category** — what money on a diary entry was spent for — service | repair | tyres | other (D12)
